Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our engineering standards, sprint cycles, 100% intellectual property ownership, security compliance, and SLA guarantees.

What is RC Techno Solutions and what sets you apart?

RC Techno Solutions is an enterprise digital solutions and software engineering firm. Unlike traditional agencies that rely on templated solutions or heavy markups, we assign dedicated engineering pods led by senior software architects. We guarantee 100% intellectual property ownership, transparent bi-weekly sprint deliverables, clean architecture with zero technical debt, and a 60-day post-launch defect warranty.

Where is RC Techno Solutions based and how do you collaborate with remote clients?

Our development operations are headquartered in Ahmedabad, India, with global clients across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. We use a transparent async-first engineering workflow: private Slack or WhatsApp channels for real-time team collaboration, continuous Jira/Linear boards, and weekly live demo milestones scheduled in your time zone.

What software development services do you offer?

We offer 14 specialized software development services: Website Development, Web Application Development, Custom Software Development, E-Commerce Development, Enterprise Software Solutions, AI Integration & Automation, AI Agents & LLM Workflows, Data Analytics & Dashboards, Database Design, Business Management Systems, API Development & Integration, Cloud Solutions, Cybersecurity, and Technical Consulting.

Can you take over and modernize an existing legacy codebase?

Yes. We frequently conduct code health and security audits on existing legacy platforms. We then implement a phased strangler-fig modernization pattern: migrating monoliths into performant microservices, upgrading deprecated dependencies, containerizing the runtime, and optimizing database queries without interrupting current operations.

What technology stacks and programming languages do you use?

Our core stack consists of modern, battle-tested technologies: React 19, Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Python (FastAPI / Django), Go, PostgreSQL Multi-AZ, MongoDB Atlas, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, and Google Cloud Platform.

Can you engineer custom AI agents and private RAG workflows?

Absolutely. We architect autonomous multi-agent pipelines with LangChain and LlamaIndex, semantic vector search using pgvector and Pinecone, and private LLM fine-tuning. Crucially, we enforce strict VPC isolation and PII redaction guardrails ensuring your enterprise proprietary data never leaks into public model training sets.

How fast can you build and ship an MVP or enterprise platform?

Rapid Minimum Viable Products (MVPs) typically ship within 3 to 6 weeks. Full-scale enterprise web applications, custom SaaS platforms, and multi-tenant systems typically launch within 8 to 12 weeks, deployed incrementally through bi-weekly staging releases.

How frequently will we receive progress updates and staging demos?

We operate on 2-week agile sprints. You receive continuous staging access where you can test features as they are approved by automated QA. At the end of every sprint, we conduct a live milestone walkthrough with your stakeholders.

How are your software development projects priced?

We offer two transparent pricing models: Fixed-Scope Milestone Contracts for clearly defined MVPs and platforms with locked specifications; and Dedicated Dedicated Pod Retainers for continuous sprint development, scaling, and architectural evolution with flexible monthly allocation.

What are your payment terms and milestone structures?

For fixed-scope projects, payments are tied directly to verifiable deliverable milestones (e.g. 25% project kick-off, 25% architecture & core UI staging, 25% full API & integration testing, 25% production deployment and source handover). No surprise fees or hidden licensing costs.

Who owns the source code and intellectual property?

You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, databases, and assets from Day 1. All code is committed directly to your private Git repositories (GitHub/GitLab) or transferred fully upon completion. RC Techno Solutions retains zero rights and zero royalties.

Do we get full access to the cloud hosting environments?

Yes. All infrastructure is deployed via declarative Terraform or Docker files into your company's own AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure cloud tenancy. You retain root credentials, IAM ownership, and full administrative control at all times.

Do you execute mutual NDAs prior to technical discovery calls?

Yes. It is our standard operating practice. You can generate and execute an instant bilateral mutual non-disclosure agreement directly on our website via our Instant NDA Portal, or we can sign your organization's standard corporate NDA prior to examining proprietary documents.

What cybersecurity practices do you implement in applications?

Every platform is engineered according to OWASP Top 10 security standards: TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, parameterized SQL queries preventing injection, OAuth 2.0 / JWT session validation, role-based access control (RBAC), and automated dependency vulnerability scans.

What is included in your 60-Day SLA Warranty?

Every custom software build we deliver comes with an automatic 60-day post-launch warranty at zero additional cost. Any bug, defect, or performance issue related to the agreed scope is resolved immediately with priority triage and fast-track deployment.

Do you provide ongoing cloud maintenance and 24/7 SLA retainers?

Yes. For enterprise clients requiring guaranteed uptime and ongoing feature development, we offer flexible monthly maintenance retainers including 24/7 uptime monitoring, automated database backups, security patching, and dedicated sprint hours.
